DIP DIET - CORE PRINCIPLES AND OVERVIEW
=======================================

The DIP Diet (Disciplined & Intelligent Plan) was developed by Dr. Biswaroop Roy Chowdhury.
It is a plant-based, whole-food dietary system designed to reverse chronic diseases naturally
through food as medicine.

CORE PHILOSOPHY
---------------
The DIP Diet is based on the premise that the human body is self-healing when provided with
the right nutrition. It emphasizes:

1. RAW FOOD DOMINANCE: At least 50% of every meal should be raw, uncooked food (salads, fruits).
2. WHOLE FOODS ONLY: No processed, packaged, or refined foods.
3. PLANT-BASED: No animal products, dairy, eggs, or meat.
4. MEAL TIMING: Strict adherence to meal windows for maximum benefit.
5. FOOD AS MEDICINE: Specific foods prescribed for specific conditions.

THE THREE PILLARS OF DIP DIET
------------------------------
Pillar 1 - DISCIPLINED EATING:
- Eat only during designated meal windows
- No snacking between meals (except approved items)
- Chew food thoroughly (at least 32 times per bite)
- Eat in a relaxed, calm environment
- No screen time during meals

Pillar 2 - INTELLIGENT FOOD CHOICES:
- Choose foods with highest nutrient density
- Prioritize local, seasonal, and organic produce
- Avoid foods that cause inflammation
- Understand the glycemic index and choose accordingly
- Combine foods wisely for optimal digestion

Pillar 3 - PLANNED LIFESTYLE:
- Regular sleep schedule (10 PM to 5:30 AM)
- Daily physical activity (minimum 45 minutes)
- Sunlight exposure (20-30 minutes daily)
- Stress management through breathing exercises
- Earthing (barefoot contact with natural ground)

PROHIBITED FOODS (STRICTLY AVOIDED)
-------------------------------------
1. All dairy products (milk, curd, paneer, ghee, butter, cheese, cream)
2. All animal products (meat, fish, eggs, poultry)
3. All refined grains (white rice, maida/all-purpose flour, semolina/suji)
4. All sugar and sweeteners (white sugar, brown sugar, honey, jaggery, artificial sweeteners)
5. All packaged and processed foods
6. All tea and coffee (including green tea)
7. All alcohol and carbonated beverages
8. Deep-fried foods
9. Refined oils (use maximum 1 teaspoon of cold-pressed oil per day)

APPROVED FOODS
--------------
1. All fresh fruits (seasonal and local preferred)
2. All raw vegetables
3. Whole grains (brown rice, millets, jowar, bajra, ragi, foxtail millet, wheat)
4. Legumes and pulses (all dals, chana, rajma, moong, masoor)
5. Nuts and seeds (almonds, walnuts, flaxseeds, chia seeds, sunflower seeds)
6. Cold-pressed oils in minimum quantity (coconut oil, sesame oil)
7. Spices and herbs (turmeric, cumin, coriander, ginger, garlic)
8. Coconut water and fresh coconut
9. Sprouts (moong sprouts, chana sprouts, methi sprouts)

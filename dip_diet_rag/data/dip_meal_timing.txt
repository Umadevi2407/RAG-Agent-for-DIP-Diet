DIP DIET - MEAL PLAN AND TIMING GUIDELINES
==========================================

DAILY MEAL SCHEDULE
-------------------

BREAKFAST (7:00 AM - 9:00 AM):
- ONLY fresh fruits are consumed during this window
- Minimum 2-3 varieties of seasonal fruits
- Quantity: eat until satisfied (no restriction)
- No cooked food during breakfast
- Examples:
  * Watermelon + Banana + Apple + Papaya
  * Mango + Pomegranate + Guava + Grapes
  * Orange + Pear + Chikoo (Sapota) + Seasonal berries
  * Mixed fruit bowl with 5-6 varieties

Why fruit breakfast?
- Morning is the body's detox window (5 AM to 12 PM)
- Fruits are easily digestible and don't disrupt detox
- High water content helps flush toxins
- Natural sugars provide quick, clean energy

LUNCH (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM):
- TWO-PLATE SYSTEM (mandatory)
- Plate 1 (Raw Salad - 50% of meal volume):
  * Cucumber, tomato, carrot, beetroot, radish, capsicum
  * Leafy greens: spinach, cabbage, lettuce, coriander
  * Dressed with only lemon juice and black pepper
  * NO salt, NO oil dressings
- Plate 2 (Cooked Food - 50% of meal volume):
  * One whole grain (rice, millet, roti)
  * One dal/legume preparation
  * Optional: one cooked vegetable sabzi

Lunch Examples:
- Plate 1: Cucumber + Tomato + Carrot + Onion (lemon dressing)
  Plate 2: Brown rice + Moong dal + Ridge gourd sabzi

- Plate 1: Cabbage + Beetroot + Cucumber + Coriander
  Plate 2: Jowar roti + Chana dal

- Plate 1: Spinach + Radish + Tomato + Capsicum
  Plate 2: Ragi roti + Toor dal + Drumstick

DINNER (6:00 PM - 8:00 PM):
- Same TWO-PLATE SYSTEM as lunch
- Dinner should be lighter than lunch
- Finish eating by 8:00 PM (hard rule)
- Same structure: 50% raw salad + 50% cooked

Dinner Examples:
- Plate 1: Beetroot + Capsicum + Cucumber (lemon)
  Plate 2: Bajra roti + Bottle gourd sabzi

- Plate 1: Carrot + Radish + Tomato
  Plate 2: Foxtail millet khichdi

SNACKS (Mid-morning / Mid-afternoon):
- Coconut water (best snack on DIP Diet)
- Fresh coconut pieces
- Soaked almonds (5-7 pieces)
- 2 walnuts
- Mixed sprouts with lemon
- Seasonal fruit
- Chia seeds soaked in water
- Flaxseed water

FOOD TIMINGS - DO'S AND DON'TS
--------------------------------
1. Eat breakfast within 1-2 hours of waking up
2. Gap between breakfast and lunch: minimum 3 hours
3. Gap between lunch and dinner: minimum 4 hours
4. No eating after 8 PM
5. Drink water 30 minutes BEFORE meals
6. Do NOT drink water for 45 minutes AFTER meals
7. Total water intake: 8-10 glasses per day
8. Prefer warm or room temperature water

MEAL PREPARATION GUIDELINES
-----------------------------
1. Steaming preferred over boiling (preserves nutrients)
2. Maximum 1 teaspoon cold-pressed oil per day total
3. Use spices freely (turmeric, cumin, coriander, ginger, garlic, asafoetida)
4. Avoid pressure cooking when possible (destroys enzymes)
5. Soak grains and legumes overnight before cooking
6. Sprout where possible (increases nutrition by 300-500%)
7. Cook at lower temperatures to preserve nutrients
8. Avoid microwaving food
